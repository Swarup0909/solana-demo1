## ⚠️ Important Notes

### USDC vs SOL Pairs

**USDC Pairs** (Recommended):
- ✅ Shows accurate USD price
- ✅ Easy to understand
- ✅ Direct price comparison
- Example: BONK-USDC shows $0.00002461

**SOL Pairs** (Works but different):
- 📊 Shows price relative to SOL
- 📊 Need SOL price to convert to USD
- 📊 Good for tracking, not direct USD
- Example: MEW-SOL shows 0.838 (MEW per SOL)

### Liquidity Requirements

For best results, choose pools with:
- ✅ TVL: $100,000+ minimum
- ✅ TVL: $1M+ ideal
- ✅ 24h Volume: $1M+ preferred
- ✅ Active trading (recent transactions)

### Pool Types

**Standard AMM (V4)** ✅ Supported:
- What we currently support
- Most common pool type
- Works perfectly

**CLMM (Concentrated Liquidity)** ⚠️ Not supported:
- Raydium's new pool type
- Different data structure
- Need code modifications

**CP (Constant Product)** ⚠️ Not supported:
- Another pool variant
- Skip these for now
